💜

PEOPLE WHO CAN SUPPORT YOU

Where can you start?

🏫

School counselor

Ask your school whether a guidance counselor or student support service is available. You can start by saying that you'd like someone to talk to.

🏡

Trusted adult

Reach out to a family member, teacher, coach, or another adult who listens respectfully and helps you feel safe.

🩺

Healthcare professional

A doctor or mental health professional can help you understand what you're experiencing and discuss suitable support.

🤝

Someone who listens

If talking feels difficult, you could write down what you want to say or ask someone you trust to sit with you while you explain.

IF YOU NEED URGENT HELP

Your immediate safety comes first.

If you are in immediate danger or experiencing a medical emergency in the Philippines, contact local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department. If possible, tell a trusted adult and ask them to stay with you while you get help.

MindCare is a student wellness project, not a substitute for professional care or emergency services.
